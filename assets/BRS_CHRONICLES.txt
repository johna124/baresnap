Chapter III — The CPU Firewall
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While bloated "Enterprise" software exhausts the CPU trying to compress files
that are already closed (multimedia, zips, isos), BareSnap executes its "Entropy
Firewall" [1.1]. A stochastic sampling of 1024 bytes directly in the RAM stack [1.1].
If it detects white noise, it turns off the compression bit on the fly, saving
clock cycles and protecting the processor [1.1].
